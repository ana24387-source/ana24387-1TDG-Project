What to do in Debate?

The most popular myth about debate is that you are just simply arguing about whose point is right, which is wrong! Debate is very structured and it is basically like a persuasive speech. As you are persuading the adjudicator to believe that your team is right.

There are many rules in debate that we help you learn in our club. They are as following:

- Your team should atleast consist of four people. As there are three total speakers in debate and to make sure nothing goes wrong you should have a reserve speaker.

- You will be given a motion hint a few days before you debate, we will help you brainstorm motion in our meetings. You get your actual motion on the debate day. Then you get 30 minutes to prep your points.

- You will be given the draw of the debate round with the motion hint, to tell you what school you will be versing. It will aslo tell you whether you are affirming or negating.

-When you are done with your preparations you then go in and wait for the adjudicator to tell you when to start.

To learn the debate rules, tips and tricks come to our meeting where Senior debaters will help you.
