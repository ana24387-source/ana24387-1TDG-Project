About Club

Do you like debating or just simply want to work on your speaking skills? Join our DEBATE CLUB. In our club we help people develop their critical thinking skills and speaking skills and help them gain some amazing experinces by helping them participate in Inter-School Debating Competetitions. You can use our website to get an overview of what we are going to learn and work on, and you can also read about past members experiences to how fun and interesting the club can be. Our club is for those students who want to work on their speaking skills and want to grow more confident, and our Debate Club can help with these.

Our school competes in Waikato Schools' Debating Annual Championships with three different leagues. One for junior debaters and two for senior debaters. It is also a paid commitment as it costs the school money to register the teams to let them enter in the Inter-school Competitions with all the other schools. The price can range from $20 to $40 per person depending on the competition entered.

If you want to know more about how debate works in our school you can visit the school website more information.
