Past Winners

Our Senior Open debaters showed real dedication this season, representing HGHS with confidence, teamwork, and pride.

Team NDL had a strong season this year, finishing with four wins from six rounds, coming incredibly close to reaching the semifinals. This highlights both the effort and the growing talent of our young debaters. This team was made up of Aarushi Nautiyal, Anahi Trikha, Chrissty Chander, and Manha Raushan, who each played an important part in the team's impressive performance.

Team 7 worked hard throughout the season, and their efforts were evident in their three well-earned wins and weekly improvement. The team demonstrated real perseverance, bouncing back from challenges, consistently pushing themselves to grow as debaters. Team 7 included Divanshi Khunger, Harini Shri Mathavan, Holly Nelson, Zeana Marie Mateo, and Maisa Sahim, who all did their best to represent our school.

We are incredibly proud of both teams and their accomplishments this year. Huge congratulations to Team NDL and Team 7 for the commitment, progress, and energy they brought to the Senior Open debates this year.
